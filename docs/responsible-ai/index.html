RESPONSIBLE AI

Useful evidence support.
Clear human accountability.

Software can retrieve and structure evidence, but qualified people must interpret it and own consequential development decisions.

Six commitments for evidence AI

01

Human autonomy

TrialLens informs review; it does not replace a development team, governance committee or regulator.

02

Safety and public interest

Metadata is never presented as proof of efficacy, safety or benefit–risk.

03

Transparency

Sources, retrieval time, automated rules, purpose and limitations are made visible.

04

Accountability

A qualified reviewer remains accountable for interpretation and the final decision.

05

Inclusiveness and equity

Reviewers should examine whether study populations represent relevant groups.

06

Responsive governance

Source changes, retrieval failures and performance issues should be monitored.

Known limitations

  • Search terms can miss synonyms or retrieve irrelevant records.
  • Registry records may be incomplete, outdated or lack posted results.
  • Publication counts do not measure quality, consistency or applicability.
  • External API availability and changes can affect retrieval.
