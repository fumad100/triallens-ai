HOW IT WORKS

From a development question
to a source-traceable review.

TrialLens retrieves public evidence directly in your browser, structures it for review and keeps every source record one click away.

Six stages, with a human decision at the end

01

Frame the review question

Start with a drug or asset and add an indication when needed.

02

Retrieve authoritative records

The browser queries ClinicalTrials.gov and NCBI PubMed directly.

03

Normalize the landscape

Records are organized by phase, status, enrollment, sponsor and result availability.

04

Assess evidence maturity

Volume and maturity signals are summarized without claiming clinical benefit.

05

Review source records

Scientists inspect endpoints, populations, relevance and missing evidence.

06

Document human judgement

The reviewer accepts the evidence set and exports a traceable brief.
